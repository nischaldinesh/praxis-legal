What is Praxis

Praxis is a mobile app for iOS and Android that helps people turn the content they save into real learning and action. People bookmark Shorts, Reels, threads, LinkedIn posts, and other interesting content as they come across them, but often never go back to them. By sharing those posts or videos to Praxis, the app keeps them in one place and reminds you to revisit them on purpose.

You can create a goal for what you want to learn or improve, then match each saved item to that goal. Once you revisit it, Praxis helps you turn it into a practice session and asks for a quick reflection note on what you learned, what stood out, and what you actually did with it.

Features

Save From Any Feed, In One Place

Share posts from any social media or the web, and find them all in one inbox.

Turn Saves Into Practice

Plan what you saved against a goal. Get reminded, take action, reflect on what you learned, and watch your progress grow.
